# BeeGlobal logo grid measurements

Source: [BeeGlobal Dev File, “test out the logos dimensions” section](https://www.figma.com/design/BAXp6TGV4E7ZJsa0RBK9eT/BeeGlobal-%7C-Dev-File?node-id=7611-1962). Measured from Figma metadata on 19 October 2026. Dimensions below are the immediate logo **frame**, not necessarily the visible artwork bounds.

Each card is 280 × 192 px. Every listed logo frame is centered in its card to the precision reported by Figma. Card positions use a 312 px horizontal and 224 px vertical step, giving 32 px gaps. The parent frame is 2776 × 640 px, with nine cards in each of the first two rows and four in the third.
